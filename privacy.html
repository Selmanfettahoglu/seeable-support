SeeAble Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

SeeAble (“we”, “the app”) is an accessibility app for blind and low-vision users. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information the app uses

  • Camera photos / frames — used to describe surroundings, answer questions, or read text. Images are sent to AI providers you select in the app (for example Ollama or OpenAI) to generate a response.
  • Microphone audio — used when you ask a voice question. Audio may be transcribed by a speech service (such as OpenAI Whisper) so the app can understand your question.
  • Optional API keys — if you enter your own OpenAI key, it is stored only on your device and sent with your requests so the provider can process them. We do not host your key on a SeeAble account system.
  • App preferences — settings such as speech speed, volume, and voice choice are stored on your device.

Accounts

SeeAble does not require creating an account or signing in to use the app.

Data sharing

Content you submit (photos/audio/text) is processed by the AI provider path you choose in Settings so the app can return an answer. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies. SeeAble does not sell your personal information.

Data retention

The app may keep a recent description or session state on your device to support features like repeating an answer. Server-side retention depends on the AI provider and free-tier service configuration you use.

Children’s privacy

SeeAble is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit personal information about children through the app.
