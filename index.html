SeeAble Support

SeeAble helps blind and low-vision users ask about what’s in front of the camera by voice, or point at a page and hear text read aloud.

Important safety note

SeeAble is not a navigation or safety tool. Do not rely on it alone. Always use your mobility aids, your own judgment, or trusted human assistance. AI answers can be incomplete or wrong. Do not use SeeAble to confirm medication, allergens, identity, or whether it is safe to walk, cross, eat, drink, or touch something.
